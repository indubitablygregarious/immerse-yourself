# 2026-02-24

Big sound engine day — added three new features back to back. Started with fadeout support for sound_conf sounds so one-shot effects can trail off naturally instead of cutting abruptly. Then built retrigger mode for the atmosphere engine, which lets sounds fire at random intervals instead of looping continuously — think occasional bird calls or a rooster crowing rather than a constant loop. Added a deferred initial play so retriggered sounds wait before their first trigger instead of all firing the instant you load an environment. Also added start_offset for atmosphere sounds so you can skip past dead air or intros on longer clips. All of this came from a mix of prepping environments for an upcoming D&D session and noticing issues while testing — one thing led to another. Swapped out the Fey Wild SFX for proper ambient sounds and fixed the `make dev-full` content sync while I was at it.
